# Evolutionary path-based identification of DCC1-DCC7

For each dominant circulating clone (DCC), the corresponding clade was identified from the subspecies-specific maximum-likelihood phylogeny. Large clades were pruned with Treemmer while retaining at least 70% of the original phylogenetic diversity, and ancestral states were reconstructed with SNPPar v1.0 under a general time-reversible substitution model with gamma-distributed rate heterogeneity. Candidate clade-defining SNPs were obtained from mutations accumulated along the evolutionary path leading to the most recent common ancestor of each DCC. Because homologous recombination and deeper shared ancestry can produce markers carried by more than one lineage, candidate markers were filtered against the complete population. A SNP was retained as a DCC barcode marker when its derived allele was present in at least 95% of isolates in the target DCC and in less than 5% of isolates outside that DCC. This produced 819, 578, 257, 478, 154, 602, and 1,184 markers for DCC1-DCC7, respectively.

For prospective assignment, quality-filtered reads were mapped to the reference genome corresponding to the isolate's subspecies: GZ002 (NZ_CP034181.1) for subsp. *abscessus* and CCUG 48898 (NZ_AP014547.1) for subsp. *massiliense*. Consensus alleles were generated with VarScan2, and the proportion of lineage-defining derived alleles detected in each isolate was calculated. Subsp. *abscessus* isolates were evaluated against DCC1, DCC2, DCC4, and DCC5, whereas subsp. *massiliense* isolates were evaluated against DCC3, DCC6, and DCC7. An isolate was assigned to the best-matching DCC when at least 95% of that DCC's barcode SNPs were detected; otherwise, it was classified as non-DCC.

This implementation is restricted to the seven previously recognized DCCs. It does not identify emerging DCCs and does not support subsp. *bolletii*, for which none of DCC1-DCC7 is defined.
